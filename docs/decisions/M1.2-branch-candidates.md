---

## Context

M1.1 established the following finding:

> No exploration area has any internal implementation, prototype,
> research artifact, or architecture document behind it.

Each of the six areas is a name and a one-sentence description.

M1.2 applies the candidate framework to this evidence.

---

## Candidate Requirements (from brief)

A candidate must have evidence for at least:

| Requirement       | Definition                                              |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Substance         | Something real exists                                   |
| Coherence         | The work forms a recognizable capability or theme       |
| Aryntra relevance | It genuinely belongs to Aryntra's long-term identity    |
| Demonstrability   | There is something that could eventually be shown       |
| Growth potential  | The area can grow beyond one isolated experiment        |
| Public clarity    | A stranger could understand it without explanation      |

## Candidate A — Decision Intelligence

### What it is

Systems that help people understand complex situations and make
informed decisions with confidence.

### Existing substance

One content definition in exploration.ts.
The philosophy.ts pipeline (Observe → Understand → Reason → Decide →
Create → Act) provides a conceptual framework that maps most directly
to this area.

### Evidence

- exploration.ts id: "decision" — one sentence
- philosophy.ts pipeline — six-step cognitive model
- No repository, prototype, or research artifact

### Why Aryntra

Decision intelligence is the area most directly stated in Aryntra's
positioning. The tagline is "India's Intelligent Decision Layer."
The word "decision" appears in the company name layer itself.
Strategic alignment is genuine.

### Public expression

Nothing beyond the existing homepage text.

### Risks

Selecting this area as a branch based on tagline alignment alone
would be selecting a branch because the website needs more content —
the hard stop condition from the brief. The strategic fit is real.
The substance is not yet real.

### Score

| Criterion         | Score | Reasoning                                              |
| ----------------- | ----: | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Genuine substance |     1 | Content definition only. No implementation.            |
| Strategic fit     |     5 | Core of Aryntra's stated identity                      |
| Demonstrability   |     1 | Nothing to demonstrate beyond the description          |
| Public clarity    |     3 | The concept is understandable to a general audience    |
| Growth potential  |     3 | Direction is broad enough to grow significantly        |
| **Total**         | **13** |                                                       |

### Classification

**Potential candidate** — strategic fit is exceptional but substance
score of 1 disqualifies it from Strong candidate status.

## Recommended Candidate(s)

**Decision Intelligence** scores highest (13/25) and is the only area
with exceptional strategic fit (5/5). It maps directly to Aryntra's
tagline, positioning, and the philosophy pipeline.

However: every candidate received a Genuine Substance score of 1/5.

The branch decision threshold requires Genuine Substance ≥ 4/5.

No candidate currently clears that threshold.

---

## Deferred Areas

**Spatial Intelligence** and **Creation Intelligence** are classified as
not candidates at this stage. They are not dismissed permanently —
they are deferred until there is internal work behind them.

**Decision Intelligence** is identified as the area most worth
developing internally first, specifically because strategic fit is
already exceptional. If internal work begins there, it would reach
branch readiness before any other area.

---

## Conclusion

M1.2 finds four potential candidates and no strong candidates.

The deciding factor is identical across all six areas:
Genuine substance score of 1/5.

This finding is the correct and complete input for M1.3.
