consistent, the content is honest, the architecture is clean. No fake products,

no fake ecosystem, no speculative infrastructure.

live-site review. Two were silent rendering bugs. Two were content and

documentation inaccuracies.

\## 8. M1 Decision



> \*\*M1 should not branch yet. The trunk needs one more strengthening pass.\*\*

>

> Specifically: identify which of the six exploration areas has the most

> genuine internal work behind it. Give that area enough surface area —

> either on the homepage or as a minimal dedicated page — to feel real

> rather than conceptual. This is not a projects dashboard. It is one

> focused, honest signal that Aryntra is building something specific.

>

> If no exploration area has sufficient internal substance to present

> honestly, M1 should be scoped to continued trunk strengthening:

> favicon, CI pipeline, and contact infrastructure.
