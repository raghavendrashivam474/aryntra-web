---

## Problem

Engineering teams make architecture decisions under time pressure,
incomplete information, and competing constraints.

They currently do this through discussion, instinct, and ad-hoc
comparison. The reasoning is rarely made explicit. The trade-offs
are rarely named before commitment. The decision logic is rarely
preserved after the fact.

The consequence: the same decision gets made again under different
people, the trade-off that was accepted is forgotten, and failures
cannot be traced back to their decision origin.

---

## Purpose

Aryntra Decision Intelligence is a structured reasoning layer that:

1. Makes constraints explicit before a decision is made
2. Evaluates alternatives against those constraints with weights
3. Identifies the trade-off being accepted, not just the winner
4. Flags uncertainty rather than hiding it in a confident output
5. Produces a reasoning trace that survives the decision

The system does not make decisions. It makes the reasoning visible.

## Public Positioning

Aryntra Decision Intelligence is a research branch exploring how
structured constraint reasoning can improve technical decision-making.

It is not a product. It is not a general AI assistant.
It is a working prototype of a specific, bounded reasoning system.

The public description must reflect what exists:

> Aryntra is building a structured reasoning layer for engineering
> decisions. The first prototype evaluates alternatives against
> weighted constraints, surfaces trade-offs explicitly, and produces
> a transparent recommendation trace. It runs today. It requires
> no AI model.
